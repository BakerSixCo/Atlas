## Welcome to Atlas

Atlas Jiu-Jitsu is a no-gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy focused on technical excellence, safety, and personal growth. Whether you're here for self-defense, competition, or fitness, we're committed to helping you reach your goals in a clean, respectful, high-performance environment.

## Mission

To provide world-class no-gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu instruction in a safe, inclusive, and disciplined environment. We emphasize respect, humility, and continuous improvement — on and off the mats.

## Core values

- **Respect** — for instructors, training partners, and the art.
- **Safety** — through hygiene, controlled sparring, and injury prevention.
- **Growth** — technical progression and personal development.
- **Community** — supporting one another through shared goals and values.

## Mat etiquette

- No shoes on the mat. Wear sandals off the mat.
- Arrive on time. If you're late, wait for the instructor's permission to join.
- No coaching from the sidelines unless asked.
- Always thank your partner after rolling.
- Be mindful of space and others during drills and sparring.

## What to wear (no-gi)

### Required

- **Rashguard** — clean, snug-fitting, moisture-wicking. Long sleeves preferred for hygiene and skin protection.
- **Grappling shorts** — made for no-gi: no pockets, zippers, buttons, or metal loops; secure waistband; durable, flexible material.
- **Spats** (compression pants) — optional but encouraged. Wear under shorts.

### Not allowed

- Loose athletic shorts (e.g. basketball shorts)
- Shorts with pockets or hardware
- Tank tops, cotton t-shirts, or sleeveless shirts
- Jewelry, watches, piercings, or accessories
- Dirty, torn, or smelly gear

> **First class?** Athletic clothes with no zippers, pockets, or buttons are fine for your first visit.

## Hygiene & health

- Shower immediately after training with antibacterial soap (e.g. Defense Soap, Hibiclens).
- Keep nails trimmed and clean.
- Use deodorant and maintain oral hygiene.
- Wash all gear after every session.
- Do not train if you're sick or have open wounds.

### Skin conditions to watch for

- **Ringworm** — fungal infection causing red, itchy patches.
- **Staph (including MRSA)** — bacterial infection that can be serious if untreated.
- **Impetigo** — highly contagious bacterial skin infection.
- **Herpes gladiatorum** — viral infection common in contact sports.
- **Folliculitis** — inflamed hair follicles from friction or bacteria.

If you suspect any of these, tell an instructor and see a doctor right away. Do not train until you're cleared.

## Training guidelines

- Tap early, tap often. Respect the tap.
- Train with control, not aggression.
- Match intensity to your partner's level.
- Ask before rolling with someone new.
- Do not teach unless authorized by an instructor.
- No slamming, neck cranks, or twisting leg locks unless permitted in advanced classes.

## Rank & promotion

Atlas uses a no-gi ranking system based on skill, time on the mat, and contribution to the academy. Promotions are earned through:

- Technical proficiency
- Consistent attendance
- Positive attitude and teamwork
- Competition performance (optional)

*Asking for a promotion may delay it.*

## Class structure

- **Fundamentals** — core techniques, movement, and positional control.
- **Advanced** — strategy, submissions, transitions, and leg lock systems.
- **Open Mat** — informal training. Respect others' space and intensity.
- **Competition Prep** — high-intensity drilling and situational sparring.

## Code of conduct

- Be inclusive and supportive of all students.
- Leave personal conflicts outside the academy.
- No bullying, harassment, or discrimination.
- Help keep the space clean — pick up after yourself.
- Represent Atlas Jiu-Jitsu with pride and humility.
