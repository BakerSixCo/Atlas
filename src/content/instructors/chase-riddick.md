Chase is a black belt under the lineage of the late Keith Owen, a respected Pedro Sauer black belt. After Keith's passing, the journey continued — with black belt promotion awarded by Josh Arentz, one of his most senior students.

Chase created Atlas with a clear vision: to build a non-traditional studio that's free from politics, drama, and ego — a place where people simply train hard and love the art of jiu-jitsu.
