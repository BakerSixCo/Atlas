## How open mat works

Open mat is informal training. Pair up, pick what you want to work on, and go. Respect others' space and intensity, ask before rolling with someone new, and match your partner's pace.

## Who it's for

Everyone. Newer students use it to drill fundamentals; experienced students use it for extra rounds or competition prep. Visitors from other gyms are welcome — [contact us](/contact) first.
