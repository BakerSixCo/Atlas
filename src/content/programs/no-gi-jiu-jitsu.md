## What a class looks like

Every no-gi class at Atlas runs 60 minutes:

1. **Warm-up** — movement drills that build the hip escapes, rolls, and base you'll use all class.
2. **Technique** — a focused position or sequence, broken down step by step.
3. **Drilling** — reps with a partner at a controlled pace until the technique sticks.
4. **Live rounds** — optional sparring, matched to your level and intensity.

## Why train no-gi at Atlas

Atlas specializes in no-gi. It's not an extra class tacked onto a gi program — it's everything we do. That means the curriculum, the drilling, and the live rounds are all built around modern no-gi grappling: control, takedowns, guard work, and submissions, including the leg lock systems taught in advanced classes.

You'll train in a culture built on respect over rank: no politics, no drama, no egos. And your membership includes 24/7 access to The Mecca Gym for strength and conditioning.
