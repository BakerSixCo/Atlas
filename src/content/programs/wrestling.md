## Why wrestling makes better grapplers

Most jiu-jitsu players avoid the standup game. That's exactly why it's an advantage. Wrestling teaches you to dictate where the fight goes: take it down, stay on top, or scramble back to your feet.

## What you'll train

- Stance, motion, and hand-fighting
- Single legs, double legs, and finishes
- Front headlocks and go-behinds
- Scrambles and getting back to your feet
- Safe falling and takedown defense

Pair it with [no-gi jiu-jitsu](/programs/no-gi-jiu-jitsu) for a complete grappling game.
