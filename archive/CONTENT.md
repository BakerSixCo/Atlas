## Student handbook

The full text is from `assets/Atlas_Jiu_Jitsu_Student_Handbook-1.pdf`.

**Welcome.** Atlas Jiu Jitsu is a no-gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy focused on technical excellence, safety, and personal growth. Whether you're here for self-defense, competition, or fitness, we're committed to helping you reach your goals in a clean, respectful, and high-performance environment.

**Mission.** To provide world-class no-gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu instruction in a safe, inclusive, and disciplined environment. We emphasize respect, humility, and continuous improvement, on and off the mats.

**Core values.**
- Respect: for instructors, training partners, and the art.
- Safety: through hygiene, controlled sparring, and injury prevention.
- Growth: technical progression and personal development.
- Community: supporting one another through shared goals and values.

**Etiquette on the mat.**
- No shoes on the mat; wear sandals off the mat.
- Arrive on time. If late, wait for instructor permission to join.
- No coaching from the sidelines unless asked.
- Always thank your partner after rolling.
- Be mindful of space and others during drills and sparring.

**Required gear (no-gi only).**
- Rashguards: clean, snug-fitting, moisture-wicking. Long sleeves preferred.
- Grappling shorts: designed for no-gi, with no pockets, zippers, buttons or metal loops, a secure waistband, and durable, flexible material.
- Spats: optional but encouraged, worn under shorts.

**Prohibited.** Loose athletic shorts (e.g., basketball shorts); shorts with pockets or hardware; tank tops, cotton t-shirts, sleeveless shirts; jewelry, watches, piercings, accessories; dirty, torn or smelly gear.

**Personal hygiene.**
- Shower immediately after training with antibacterial soap (e.g., Defense Soap, Hibiclens).
- Keep nails trimmed and clean.
- Use deodorant and maintain oral hygiene.
- Wash all gear after every session.
- Do not train if you're sick or have open wounds.

**Skin conditions to watch for.** Ringworm, staph (including MRSA), impetigo, herpes gladiatorum, folliculitis. If you suspect any of these, notify an instructor and seek medical attention immediately. Do not train until cleared.

**Training guidelines.**
- Tap early, tap often. Respect the tap.
- Train with control, not aggression.
- Match intensity to your partner's level.
- Ask before rolling with someone new.
- Do not teach unless authorized by an instructor.
- No slamming, neck cranks, or twisting leg locks unless permitted in advanced classes.

**Rank and promotion.** No-gi ranking based on skill, mat time, and contribution to the academy. Promotions are earned through technical proficiency, consistent attendance, positive attitude and teamwork, and competition performance (optional). *Asking for promotions may delay them.*

**Class structure.**
- Fundamentals: core techniques, movement, positional control.
- Advanced: strategy, submissions, transitions, leg lock systems.
- Open Mat: informal training; respect others' space and intensity.
- Competition Prep: high-intensity drilling and situational sparring.

**Code of conduct.**
- Be inclusive and supportive of all students.
- Leave personal conflicts outside the academy.
- No bullying, harassment, or discrimination.
- Help maintain cleanliness: pick up after yourself.
- Represent Atlas Jiu Jitsu with pride and humility.

**Handbook FAQ.**
- **Too old to start?** Absolutely not. BJJ is for all ages and fitness levels.
- **Need to be in shape first?** No. Training will help you get in shape.
- **Can I visit other gyms?** Yes, but always ask permission and follow their etiquette.
- **Injured or sick?** Notify your instructor immediately and rest until fully recovered.

## Problems found on the current site

Fixing these is part of the rebuild:

1. **Leftover theme demo content is still live and indexable:** `/faq/` and `/team/` (fake instructors with "bacon ipsum" bios), `/member/`, `/elements/`, `/typography/`, `/home-mma/`, `/sample-page/`, about 8 sample blog posts, and 2 fake "events" (a karate seminar in LA) published through The Events Calendar plugin.
2. **WooCommerce is installed** (shop, cart, checkout and my-account pages) but nothing is for sale. It adds attack surface for no benefit.
3. **The schedule contradicts itself** between the home page and the schedule page (see above).
4. **The brand name is misspelled** ("JUI-JITSU") in the hero and in the filename `mecca-jui-jitsu`.
5. **A kids program is advertised** in the FAQ and meta description but isn't on the schedule.
6. **The "free trial" in the meta description** isn't offered anywhere on the page.
7. **"Read more" for the instructor** leads to no bio.
8. **The site exposes `xmlrpc.php`** and an open REST API (which is how this archive was pulled). Both are common WordPress attack vectors.
9. **Most image files are named `Untitled-design-NN.png` and have no alt text,** which hurts accessibility and SEO.
